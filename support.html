About the app

FOMO Stock Game is a virtual stock & crypto trading game for entertainment. All trades use virtual money with no real monetary value. It is not real investing and nothing in the app is financial advice.

Frequently asked

  • Is this real money? No. All cash, trades, and rewards are virtual and have no real-world value.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and cloud data.
  • My game data didn't sync / I lost progress. Make sure you are signed in on the same account, then reopen the app. If it persists, email us with your game ID.
  • Prices look delayed. Market prices come from public data sources and may be slightly delayed.
